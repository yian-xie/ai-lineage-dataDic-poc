How to read descriptions, relationships and confidence

Source descriptions and Snowflake descriptions are supplied metadata. They do not establish a reviewed business definition. Unknown information stays unknown. Relationship scores describe the recorded assessment of a specific operation; an upstream model self-assessment, checklist result, human assessment and historical lookup are different origins. Scores are not statistical probabilities or approval. A missing numeric assessment is Unscored, including when an original mapping has a text label such as explicit or confirmed.
